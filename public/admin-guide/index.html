Admin Dashboard Guide

A step-by-step reference for managing the Animal SOS website. No technical knowledge required. If anything on this page is unclear, contact the developer — details at the bottom.

Getting Started

Accessing the admin panel

The admin panel is a private area of the website — visitors cannot see it. To open it, go to:

https://[your-domain]/admin

Replace [your-domain] with the actual website address once it is live. Bookmark this URL so you can find it quickly.

ℹ
Tip: The admin panel works on any device — phone, tablet, or computer. A larger screen is more comfortable for managing animals and applications.

Logging in

  1. Open the admin panel URL in your browser.
  2. Enter your email address and password.
  3. Click Login.

⚠
Forgot your password? Click Forgot password on the login screen and enter your email. A reset link will be sent to you. If you do not receive it within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

Animals

The Animals section controls everything the public sees on the adoption listing page — photos, descriptions, personality details, and availability status.

Adding a new animal

  1. Click Animals in the left sidebar.
  2. Click the Create New button in the top-right corner.
  3. Fill in the fields (see the field guide below).
  4. Upload a photo — see Uploading photos below.
  5. Click Save in the top-right corner.
✓
The animal will appear on the website immediately after saving. If you want to prepare a listing without publishing it yet, leave the Status field set to Draft before saving.

Animal fields explained

FieldWhat to enter
NameThe animal's name (e.g. Bruno, Luna)REQUIRED

BreedBreed name, or Mixed breed if unknown

SizeSelect Small, Medium, or Large

ColourBrief colour description (e.g. brown and white)

VaccinatedTick if the animal has received vaccinations
Neutered / SpayedTick if the animal has been neutered or spayed
MicrochippedTick if the animal has been microchipped
StatusSet to Available to show on the website. Set to Draft to hide it. Set to Adopted when the animal has found a home.REQUIRED

Editing an existing animal

  1. Click Animals in the sidebar.
  2. Find the animal in the list and click on its name to open it.
  3. Make your changes to any field.
  4. Click Save in the top-right corner.
ℹ
Changes appear on the public website within a few seconds of saving. There is no separate "publish" step — saving is publishing.

Uploading photos

  1. On the animal edit page, find the Photo field.
  2. Click Add relationship or the upload icon.
  3. Click Upload a new file.
  4. Select the photo from your computer or phone.
  5. In the Alt text field, type a short description of the photo (e.g. Bruno sitting in the garden). This is required for accessibility.
  6. Click Save to confirm the upload.
  7. The photo will appear in the Photo field — click Save on the animal record to confirm.
✓
Best photo tips: Use a photo taken in natural daylight. Make sure the animal's face is clearly visible. Photos taken at eye level with the animal work best. Avoid dark or blurry photos — they reduce adoption enquiries.
⚠
Alt text is required. If you skip the Alt text field the photo will not save. Type a brief plain-English description of what is in the photo.

Hiding an animal listing (marking as adopted or draft)

  1. Open the animal record.
  2. Change the Status field:
    — Adopted = animal has found a home (removed from public listing)
    — Draft = temporarily hidden (useful while you gather photos or write the description)
  3. Click Save.

Adoption Applications

Every time someone submits an adoption application on the website, a record appears here. You will also receive an email notification automatically.

Viewing applications

  1. Click Adoptions in the sidebar.
  2. You will see a list of all applications, newest first.
  3. Click any application to open it and read the full details.

Each application shows:

FieldWhat it contains
Applicant nameFull name of the person applying
EmailTheir contact email address
PhoneTheir phone number
AnimalWhich animal they are applying for
Home typeHouse, apartment, etc.
Has gardenWhether their home has outdoor space
Other petsWhat other animals they currently have
ExperienceTheir experience with animals
Why adoptTheir personal reason for adopting
StatusCurrent stage of the application
Reference numberUnique code for this application (e.g. SOS-20240518-A3F)

Updating application status

As you process each application, update its status to keep records organised and keep the applicant informed:

StatusWhen to use it
PendingNew application — not yet reviewed
Under reviewYou have read it and are considering it
Home check scheduledYou have arranged a home visit
ApprovedThe application has been accepted
RejectedThe application was not suitable
CompletedThe animal has been collected and rehomed
  1. Open the application record.
  2. Click the Status dropdown and select the correct stage.
  3. Click Save.
ℹ
Updating the status does not automatically send an email to the applicant. Contact them directly using the email address on their application.

Success Stories

Success stories appear on the Success Stories page of the website. They inspire potential adopters and celebrate animals who have found their forever home.

Adding a new success story

  1. Click Success Stories in the sidebar.
  2. Click Create New.
  3. Fill in the fields:
FieldWhat to enter
Animal nameThe animal's nameREQUIRED
Adopter nameFirst name of the adopter (get their permission before publishing)
StoryA short, warm account of the adoption — how they met, what life is like nowREQUIRED
PhotoA photo of the animal in their new home, or with their adopterREQUIRED
DateThe approximate date of adoption
  1. Click Save. The story will appear on the website immediately.
✓
Ask adopters to send a photo. An after-adoption photo of the animal in their new home is one of the most powerful things on the website. When you mark an adoption as completed, send a quick email asking if they would like to share their story.

Contact Form Submissions

Messages sent through the website's contact form are saved here. You will also receive them by email, but this section keeps a permanent record.

Viewing messages

  1. Click Contacts in the sidebar.
  2. Click any message to open it.
  3. Reply to the person directly using their email address — you cannot reply from within the admin panel.
ℹ
There is no "mark as read" or "archive" feature. Once you have dealt with a message, you can leave it as-is or delete it using the Delete button at the bottom of the record.

Media Library

The Media section stores every photo and file uploaded to the website. You rarely need to visit this directly — photos are usually uploaded from within an animal record. But it is useful for finding or deleting old files.

Uploading a file directly

  1. Click Media in the sidebar.
  2. Click Create New.
  3. Click the upload area and select your file.
  4. Enter an Alt text description.
  5. Click Save.

Finding an existing photo

  1. Click Media in the sidebar.
  2. Use the search bar at the top to search by filename or alt text.
  3. Click a photo to view or edit its details.
⚠
Do not delete photos that are in use. If a photo is attached to an animal profile or success story and you delete it from the Media library, it will disappear from the website. Only delete photos that are not attached to any record.

Admin Users

This section manages who has login access to the admin panel. Only create accounts for people who genuinely need access — staff and trusted volunteers.

Adding a new admin user

  1. Click Users in the sidebar.
  2. Click Create New.
  3. Enter their name and email address.
  4. Enter a temporary password — tell them to change it after first login.
  5. Click Save.
✓
Use a strong password — at least 12 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. The admin panel controls the entire website, so access should be treated carefully.

Removing a user

  1. Open the user record.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and click Delete.
  3. Confirm the deletion.
ℹ
Remove admin access promptly when a staff member or volunteer leaves the organisation. This is an important security step.

Changing your own password

  1. Click your name or profile icon in the top-right corner of the admin panel.
  2. Select Account.
  3. Scroll to the Password field and enter a new one.
  4. Click Save.

Troubleshooting

Common issues

ProblemWhat to do
Photo will not upload Make sure you have filled in the Alt text field. This is required. Also check the file is a JPG, PNG, or WebP image under 10 MB.
Animal not appearing on website after saving Check the animal's Status field. It must be set to Available — not Draft or Adopted.
Logged out unexpectedly Admin sessions expire after a period of inactivity. Simply log in again — your work is saved.
Forgot admin panel password Click Forgot password on the login screen. A reset email will be sent to your address.
Accidentally deleted a record Deleted records cannot be recovered from the admin panel. Contact the developer who can restore data from a backup.
Website looks wrong or a page is broken Do not try to fix it yourself. Contact the developer immediately with a screenshot and the URL of the broken page.
